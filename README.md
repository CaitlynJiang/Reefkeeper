# Lucky Dive Co.

*Swim, grow coral, and photograph sea life in a cozy underwater world.*

Lucky Dive Co. is a cozy underwater VR game about caring for a reef and getting to know the life around it. Swim through a colorful seascape, meet Lucky Cat at a small ocean shop, and turn a quiet corner of the seabed into your own coral garden.

- Use your hands to swim, pick up a brush or scraper, and clean growing spaces.
- Plant and nurture different coral species, watch them grow, and arrange harvested coral around a shipwreck. Different combinations attract different marine visitors, giving you more creatures to discover.
- Frame a shot, take a photograph, and carry the printed picture back to the photo wall beside the shop.
- Earn scallops as you work, then spend them on coral and records.
- Between gardening and exploring, browse the record rack and play music on the underwater turntable.

It is an evolving, hands-on reef playground built for relaxed exploration.
