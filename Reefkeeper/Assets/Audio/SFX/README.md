# Sound cue drop folder

Place one-shot interaction sounds here, such as pickup, scrubbing and completion cues.
Include any license/source notes alongside the audio. Ambience and Music have separate folders.

brush_scrub.wav is the user-supplied, unmodified brushing recording. ReefBrushAudio
loops it during actual contact strokes at volume 0.35, with partial 3D placement.
It stops when brushing pauses, contact is lost, the brush is released, or the
practice spot is complete. Provenance/license details have not been supplied.

Lucky Cat randomly plays one of these public high-quality previews during dialogue:

- `lucky_cat_purr_meow.mp3` — [cat purring and meow by skymary](https://freesound.org/people/skymary/sounds/412016/), CC0 1.0.
- `lucky_cat_meow.mp3` — [Cat Meow by qubodup](https://freesound.org/people/qubodup/sounds/813119/), edited from a recording by Jofae, CC0 1.0.

`stone_scrape.mp3` is the public high-quality preview of
[Stone Scrape by Allan Legemaate (alegemaate)](https://freesound.org/people/alegemaate/sounds/667284/),
released under CC0 1.0. V3 uses it for the scraper with an underwater low-pass filter.
