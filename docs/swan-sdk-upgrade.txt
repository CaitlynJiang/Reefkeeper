Swimming regression follow-up / 游泳回归修复 (2026-11-19)
- Headset report: hands, pinch and journal work, but swimming does not. No ADB device was connected during this investigation.
- Reproduced a gameplay classification defect independent of SDK input: OpenPalm required index and mean outer fingertip distances above 10 cm. Small open hands could also satisfy the absolute-distance fist thresholds, continuously braking. A medium sample could remain classified as a fist after opening because of hysteresis.
- The earlier synthetic tests used oversized fingertip distances and directly injected OpenPalm=true for movement. Those checks did not cover normal/small articulated hand geometry.
- ReefHands now compares palm-to-tip distance with the finger's articulated path length. Open/fist hysteresis uses dimensionless extension ratios. At least index plus two outer fingers need valid joints; missing data cannot latch a fist. One occluded outer finger does not veto an otherwise open hand.
- Pinch distances, middle-pinch journal gesture, palm grip offsets, swim acceleration, speed limits, glide conditions and immediate fist brake behavior are unchanged.
- Tools/Validation/CheckSwimHandShapes.cs is a Unity script-run regression fixture. Run its Main in Play mode after the opening transition, with empty hands. It constructs complete XRHand joint arrays and runs the production classifier and hand-swim step; it does not inject OpenPalm/Fist values.
- Passed: both hands, rotated poses, three scale variants; open/fist/reopen and partial joint loss; forward/backward strokes, immediate braking, no restart just from opening fists, deliberate new glide, and small-jitter rejection at 30/72/90 Hz.
- Existing slow acceleration / natural stop / head-motion rejection, tool select/release and journal toggle checks also passed. The gameplay rig was enabled, active, non-Automated, with a valid view after diving.
