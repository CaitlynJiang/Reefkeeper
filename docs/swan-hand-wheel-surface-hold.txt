Combined hand interaction prototype — 2026-11-19

Two separate commits:
1. cff3c4b: right thumb + middle pinch held 0.5s toggles the existing Tools wheel.
   Release, aim with the right-hand ray, then index-pinch an acquired tool.
2. Surface hold: put an empty hand within 7cm of an existing solid collider and
   thumb + index pinch. Either hand stops body locomotion; release lets go.
   A second hand can keep holding a surface or use a brush/scraper independently.

After release, move an eligible free hand at least 8cm to rearm its original
swimming gesture. An already extended, stationary arm cannot resume the old glide.
This is a stop/hold prototype, not climbing or pulling the body along a surface.
Head and hand tracking remain live. No UI or player-facing text was added.

Scope and cost:
- Reuses existing collision geometry. Decoration without a collider cannot anchor.
- One bounded, nonallocating overlap query per new pinch; no repeated searches
  while idle/holding, no physics joints or finger rigidbodies.
- Nearby pickups and Lucky Cat take priority; triggers, rigidbodies, player rig,
  Water, UI and Ignore Raycast layers cannot become anchors.
- Releases on pinch opening, tracking loss, disabled/destroyed/moving surface,
  hand moving over 25cm away, opening a menu, focus loss or changing input mode.
- A consumed anchor pinch stays unavailable to other grab/use/talk actions until
  the fingers release, preventing accidental remote grabs after an anchor breaks.
- The approved 3cm opposing tool offsets, SDK and ordinary swim tuning are unchanged.

Validation:
- Unity compilation; CheckSurfaceHold.cs; CheckHandToolWheel.cs;
  CheckHandControls.cs. Synthetic Editor inputs, not physical headset validation.
- Actual XRI brush/scraper selection while the other hand anchors; immediate zero
  swim velocity, no stale glide after release, deliberate new movement resumes glide.
- First-commit reverse merge checked against the combined source without conflict.
  Either feature can be reverted separately. Earlier working APKs are retained.

Headset checks still needed:
Normal swimming first; wheel open/select/release; approach a solid bench/ship
surface, pinch to stop, release and deliberately swim again; then one-hand
anchoring with the other hand cleaning. Real hand-tracking comfort and performance
must be confirmed on the PICO device.
