Editable turntable and automatic tonearm - 2026-11-23

In ImmersiveOpening, Turntable - editable contains the model and Record placement
target. Move the group to reposition everything. Move/rotate Record placement
target to adjust both the light arcs and actual landing pose. Its BoxCollider
defines release tolerance. Placement light arcs is its visual child; its Scale
controls visual diameter, and VinylPlayer.Ring Rotation Speed controls rotation.
Do not move only the visual child when changing the gameplay landing position.
Runtime no longer repositions an authored turntable or placement target.

An owned record released inside the empty target seats, then the arm raises,
swings over and lowers before music fades in. Taking the record cancels cueing,
returns the arm and fades music out. There is no headshell grab target, so it
cannot compete with record selection. The light arcs hide while a record is seated.

VinylTonearm exposes Play Angle, Lift Height and Animation Seconds in the Inspector.
VinylPlayer exposes Music Fade In / Out (1.2 / 0.8 seconds). Each VinylDisplay has
a Music AudioClip field; empty falls back to the existing background track.
Put new music in Assets/Audio/Music/Vinyl. The existing audio source and purchases
are reused. No new gameplay text or UI. Scene records are 7 mm thick; printed
photos have solid 3 mm paper stock with image and caption outside the front face.
Three soft arcs use a shared 192-triangle mesh and one unlit transparent pass;
the arm uses constrained transforms without a Rigidbody or physics joint.

Automatic cue/interrupt/return, real-frame volume fades, rapid track-switch
cancellation, per-album clips and placement persistence checked in Editor.
PICO hardware grab comfort and device rendering still need a headset test.
