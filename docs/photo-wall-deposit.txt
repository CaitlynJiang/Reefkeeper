Photo wall deposit prototype — 2026-11-22

Taking a photo now creates a pending physical print, not an album entry.
Pending species are excluded from photo targeting until the print is delivered
or disappears. Existing album photos and saved files remain valid.

Carry a grabbed print within 0.4 m of a picture slot on the front of the cat's
photo wall. Any nearby slot can trigger the transfer, but the print always flies
to its own species' slot. The wall must contain that slot and be active.
XRI releases ownership before the 0.4-second eased flight. Storage and the wall
event happen only on arrival, followed by the existing 0.3-second shrinking/spin
disappearance. The wall picture develops over 0.8 seconds with a soft vertical
reveal. Both wall distances and reveal duration are Inspector parameters.

Releasing an undelivered print leaves it in place for pickup. Unattended prints
expire after 120 seconds; held prints do not expire. Missing/destroyed walls
cancel the flight without storing the photo and allow pickup again. Unstored
textures belong to prints; on delivery the album takes ownership. Photo captions
on prints and wall cards use depth-tested, single-sided font rendering.

No new panels or controls. The old immediate-success toast is now the literal
"placeholder text", pending user-authored wording. No third-party animation
package/assets were imported. The original lightweight shader uses a smoothstep
reveal and color interpolation; reference:
https://docs.unity.cn/Packages/com.unity.shadergraph%4016.0/manual/Smoothstep-Node.html

CheckPhotoDeposit.cs passed in Editor Play mode. It validates deferred storage,
pending protection, front-side/range/species routing, release behavior, single
arrival/event, saved PNG and texture lifetime. It restores the pre-test album
entry/file. Actual engine renders verified readable front captions, blank backs,
and the develop shader. Test images use a diagnostic two-color texture.
This is not headset gesture/performance validation. No APK built for this change.

Local recovery scene files and the unrelated VoiceCapture tool are not part of
this commit. PICO Live Preview and PC quality experiments were reverted at the
user's request, preserving the Lucky Dive Co. application name.
