Coral touch reaction prototype — 2026-11-19

An empty tracked palm or index fingertip entering a planted coral's touch volume
plays its existing squash-and-spring Poke animation, shows the existing Crying
care emote and pauses care progress for 3 seconds. Duration is adjustable on
CoralNurture.touchPauseSeconds. No new artwork, panels or player-facing copy.

The hand must leave by 6cm for 0.15s before another contact can fire. Contact
starts within 1.8cm. Holding a hand in place does not renew the timer. Tracking
recovery, closing a menu or releasing a tool while already touching does not
count as a new touch. Planting requires the hand to withdraw first. Either hand
can touch; occupied hands, pinch/fist/middle-pinch gestures and menus are excluded.

Only planted nursery corals are wired into this prototype. Mature nursery corals
can react without losing harvest eligibility. Loose shop stock, decorative reef
meshes, fire-coral cleaning patches and shipwreck display copies are unchanged.
Controller and desktop touch shortcuts have not been added.

Touch rest is separate from algae blocking. It never erases growth progress or
clears algae. Fertilizing waits until the rest finishes. Repeated accepted touches
reset the same short timer rather than stacking delays. After rest, an existing
care emote resumes its previous logic; touch-only emotes fade out.

Contact uses cached per-mesh resting bounds for the current growth stage, not
individual branch triangles. This is intentionally an approximate MVP volume;
reach and empty gaps between branches should be assessed on the headset. Recoil
does not shrink the contact volume and accidentally retrigger a stationary hand.
No new colliders, rigidbodies, physics queries or additional runtime cameras.

Validation: Unity compilation and Tools/Validation/CheckCoralTouch.cs passed.
Checks cover real assets, spring response, pause/resume including partial frames,
held-contact debounce, tracking/busy/menu ownership, growth-stage geometry across
shop species, algae independence, fertilizer recovery and mature harvest state.
After warmup, 1000 steady contact polls allocated zero managed bytes in the Editor.
Actual tracked-joint samples were injected for the runtime Update path; this does
not replace physical headset testing. No new APK has been built for this feature.
