Independent photo-wall vines - 2026-11-22

Replaces the misunderstood whole-frame bend prototype. Six vine groups hang
from six fixed top pivots. Each owns its original stem, leaves, matching photos
and clips. The wooden posts, beam, crossbar, feet and decorations stay fixed.
No skinned mesh, rigidbody or joint was added. Original mesh density is unchanged.
The previous generated whole-wall skin is removed; original frame colliders restored.

Tracked empty-hand palm/fingertip sweeps affect only the nearest contacted vine.
Pinching, occupied hands, menus, camera mode, tracking loss and jumps are excluded.
Each strand has an independent damped angular spring, maximum eight degrees.
One entry gives one impulse; withdraw before touching again. Resting strands sleep.
This MVP swings each complete strand about its suspension point; it does not
simulate flexible sections or cloth. Photos inherit their own vine's motion.

HandTrackingSpace now performs the normal lazy rig rebind when its cached origin
has been replaced. An Editor input failure exposed this missing rebind; the
corresponding hardware behavior still needs a new PICO test.

PhotoBoardSwayBuilder.Build migrates the old rig without replacing the source
FBX or unrelated wall content. Repeated calls preserve the independent rig.

Validation: all six independently react, neighbours and wooden frame stay fixed,
suspension stays fixed, all cards are parented to vines, springs return to rest,
occupied hands and tracking jumps are ignored. Moving photo deposit target also
checked against the actual scene without writing save data.
